Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Shorts Publisher is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Data we access

The app requests only the permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) to the channel of the account that grants it. It does not read, collect or store personal data of any other user.

Data storage and sharing

An access token is stored locally on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload videos. We do not sell, share or transfer data to third parties. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok data

For TikTok the app requests the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload, only for the owner's own account. It reads the account's open ID to identify the authorized account and uploads videos as drafts to the TikTok inbox. The TikTok access token is stored locally on the owner's computer, is used only for these uploads and is never shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
